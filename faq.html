Frequently asked questions

Who runs SixStar Replays?
An independent creator based in France. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Twitch, TikTok or Google.
Can I use GTA6 Autoclipper for my own channel?
No. GTA6 Autoclipper is our in-house tool and only publishes to the SixStar Replays accounts. There is no public sign-up.
Does the app access my TikTok or YouTube account?
No. The only accounts ever connected are the SixStar Replays accounts, by their owner. The app does not read or post to viewers' or streamers' accounts.
Is every clip checked by a person?
Yes. Clips are approved by a person before they are scheduled. The software proposes candidates; a person decides.
Why are some clips only on YouTube?
If a clip contains music, we do not post it to TikTok at all, and post it on YouTube only after a person confirms it is fine.
How do I get a clip removed?
Email roxywild228@gmail.com with the link. We remove it the same day. See For streamers.
What data do you keep about me?
Very little — see the Privacy Policy. This website itself sets no cookies and has no analytics.
