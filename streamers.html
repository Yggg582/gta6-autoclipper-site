For streamers

Our videos exist to show off your best moments and send new viewers to your Twitch channel. You stay in control of what we do with your content.

How we credit you

  • Your Twitch name is in the caption of every video made from your stream, on every platform.
  • The caption links to twitch.tv/<your channel>.
  • We never present your content as ours.

Get on the allow-list

If you stream GTA VI in English and would like us to clip you, write to roxywild228@gmail.com from an address listed on your Twitch profile, or send us a message on Twitch. Tell us if you have rules for clippers (for example, no clips with certain content) and we will follow them.

Opt out or remove a clip

  • Email roxywild228@gmail.com with your Twitch channel name and, if you want specific clips removed, their links.
  • We remove the clips from TikTok and YouTube the same day and, if you wish, take your channel off the allow-list so no new clips are made.
  • You do not need to give a reason.

Copyright and other rights holders

If you own rights in something that appears in one of our videos (for example music or artwork) and want it removed, email roxywild228@gmail.com with a link to the video and a description of the work. We remove the video the same day while we look into it. You can also use the reporting tools of TikTok or YouTube.
