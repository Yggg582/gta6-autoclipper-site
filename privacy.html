Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the GTA6 Clips Daily app and our SixStar Replays channels (together “we”, “us”, “the service”) collect, why, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It applies to this website, to the GTA6 Clips Daily application and to the videos we publish on TikTok and YouTube.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is the independent creator who operates GTA6 Clips Daily and SixStar Replays, based in France. Contact for all privacy matters: roxywild228@gmail.com.

2. Whose data this policy covers

  • The account owner — the person who connects the SixStar Replays TikTok and YouTube accounts to GTA6 Clips Daily. GTA6 Clips Daily has no other users and no public sign-up.
  • Streamers whose public Twitch broadcasts we clip.
  • Twitch chat participants whose public chat messages are read to find highlight moments.
  • People who contact us by email.
  • Visitors of this website.

We do not collect data from TikTok or YouTube users who watch our videos, and we do not access followers, comments, direct messages, watch history or analytics of any other account. We do not set cookies, trackers or analytics on this website. The home page embeds a few of our YouTube videos in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube does not store cookies for you unless you play a video, and then the Google Privacy Policy applies.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — making and crediting highlight clips of public broadcasts, finding moments in public chat, securing our service, answering messages.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — the account owner authorizes TikTok and Google access through OAuth and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — handling copyright notices and legal requests.

5. Sharing

  • Videos and captions are sent to TikTok and YouTube (Google) to be published. Their handling of data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, do not share it for advertising and do not use it to train third-party AI models.
  • We may disclose data if required by law or to respond to a valid legal request.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • Tokens, video IDs and work files are stored only on the operator's own computer, in local files and a local database protected by the operating system account. They are never published or sent to our own servers (we have none).
  • Tokens are never written to logs. Access is limited to the operator.
  • Raw stream footage, rejected clips and chat logs are deleted automatically after a few days.
  • When the TikTok or YouTube account is disconnected, or access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens and the data obtained from that platform's API within 7 days.

7. Revoking access

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions and remove GTA6 Clips Daily.
  • YouTube / Google: Google Account permissions.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to processing and data portability, and withdraw consent at any time. Streamers and chat participants can ask us to delete clips or messages concerning them. Write to roxywild228@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the authority in your country.

9. International transfers

Our own processing happens in France. TikTok and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own safeguards, described in their privacy policies.

10. Children

Our service is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child's data reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will be announced on the home page.
