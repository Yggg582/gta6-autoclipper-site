Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA6 Autoclipper (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is an internal tool operated by one independent creator to publish short clips to the operator's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who signs in

Only the operator of the app signs in with their Google Account, once, to authorize uploads to their own channel. The app has no public users and no user accounts.

What information the app accesses

  • An OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token for the operator's YouTube channel, with permission to upload videos and manage the videos the app uploaded.
  • The ID and link of each video the app uploads, returned by the YouTube Data API.

The app does not read comments, subscribers, watch history, analytics or any other YouTube data, and does not access data of other YouTube users.

How the information is used

  • The token is used only to upload approved clips (videos.insert) and to delete a clip (videos.delete), for example on a takedown request.
  • Video IDs and links are kept so the operator can see what was published and remove a clip on request.

Storage and sharing

  • The token and video IDs are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file and a local database. They are not uploaded to any server of ours.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • The app does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies, and this website does not collect any personal information.

Retention and deletion

  • The token is kept while the app is in use. Deleting the local token file removes it immediately.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. After access is revoked, or if the app's access is no longer needed, we delete the stored token and any data obtained from YouTube API Services within 7 days.
  • To ask us to delete any data, write to the contact address below.

Streamers

Clips come from public Twitch broadcasts of streamers on our allow-list. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, contact us and we will remove it the same day.

Changes

We will update the effective date above whenever this policy changes.
