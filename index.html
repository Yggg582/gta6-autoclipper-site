GTA6 Autoclipper

GTA6 Autoclipper is a small internal tool run by one independent creator. It finds the best moments of English-language GTA VI streams on Twitch, turns them into short vertical clips with subtitles, and — after a person reviews and approves each clip — publishes them as YouTube Shorts on our own YouTube channel.

This app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3).

How it works

  • Only streamers on our manually curated allow-list are clipped: streamers who gave permission, run an official clip program, or have not asked others not to clip them. Every video credits the streamer and links to their Twitch channel, and any streamer can ask to be removed.
  • Each clip is reviewed by a person before it is published. Clips with copyrighted music are not published automatically.
  • The tool uploads videos only to the channel of its operator, who signs in once with their Google Account (OAuth 2.0). It uses videos.insert to upload and videos.delete to remove a video, for example when a streamer asks us to take a clip down.
  • The tool is not a public service: there are no user accounts and no other people's YouTube data is accessed.

Takedown requests

If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, write to roxywild228@gmail.com — we remove it the same day.
