The best moments of GTA VI streams, in under a minute

SixStar Replays publishes short vertical highlights of English-language Grand Theft Auto VI streams on Twitch — the chases, crashes, fails and reactions that made chat explode — and always sends viewers back to the streamer who made the moment.

What we publish

Highlights about a minute long

One moment per video, cut so the build-up and the streamer's reaction both fit, with burned-in English subtitles.

Credit in every video

Every caption names the streamer and links to their Twitch channel. The clip exists to send viewers to them.

Reviewed by a person

No clip goes out automatically: a person watches it, checks the title and approves it before it is scheduled.

Streamers in control

We only clip streamers on our allow-list, and any streamer can ask us to stop or to remove a clip — we act the same day.

Where to watch

The tool behind it: GTA6 Autoclipper

The videos are prepared with GTA6 Autoclipper, our own software. It watches the public chat of allow-listed GTA VI streams to find the moments viewers reacted to, cuts a vertical clip with subtitles, and puts it in a review queue. After a person approves a clip, the tool uploads it to our own accounts through the official platform APIs:

  • TikTok — TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API: the video is sent to the SixStar Replays TikTok account (as a draft in the TikTok inbox, or posted directly once our app is approved for it).
  • YouTube — YouTube Data API v3 (YouTube API Services): the video is uploaded to the SixStar Replays YouTube channel.

The tool only connects the accounts of SixStar Replays. It does not offer sign-up to the public and does not read or post to anybody else's TikTok or YouTube account. Details: How it works, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.

Our rules

  • Only streamers on our manually curated allow-list are clipped.
  • Every video credits the streamer by name and links to their Twitch channel.
  • Clips with music are never posted to TikTok, and are posted elsewhere only after a person confirms it.
  • We respect daily posting limits and do not spam.
  • Takedown requests are handled the same day: roxywild228@gmail.com.
