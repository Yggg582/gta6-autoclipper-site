How it works

From a live GTA VI stream to a short video on TikTok and YouTube — and where a person stays in the loop.

  1. Allow-list. We keep a hand-curated list of English-language GTA VI streamers: streamers who gave us permission, who run an official clipping program, or whose channel rules do not forbid clipping. Nobody else is recorded.
  2. Finding moments. While an allow-listed streamer is live, GTA6 Clips Daily keeps a short rolling buffer of the public broadcast and reads the public Twitch chat. When many different viewers react at once (laughter, “no way”, “clip it”), the moment around it becomes a candidate.
  3. Making the clip. The candidate is cut to about a minute, converted to vertical 9:16 with the streamer's webcam on top, and English subtitles are added. A music detector checks the audio.
  4. Human review. A person watches every clip in our private review page, edits the title if needed, and approves or rejects it. Rejected clips are deleted.
  5. Captions and credit. For each platform the tool writes a caption with the clip title, the streamer's name and a link to their Twitch channel, plus a few hashtags.
  6. Scheduling. Approved clips are spread over the day within posting windows and daily limits for each platform.
  7. Publishing. At its time slot the clip is uploaded to the SixStar Replays account through the official API (details below). If something fails, the clip is sent to a person to upload by hand.
  8. Takedowns. If a streamer or rights holder asks, the clip is removed from every platform the same day and the streamer can be taken off the allow-list.

How we use the TikTok API

Product / scopeWhat we use it for
Login Kit — user.info.basicThe owner of the SixStar Replays TikTok account signs in once to connect the account. We read only the basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm the right account is connected.
Content Posting API — video.uploadSend an approved clip to the inbox of the SixStar Replays account as a draft. A person then opens TikTok, adds the caption and posts it.
Content Posting API — video.publishPost an approved clip directly to the SixStar Replays account with its caption, after our app is approved for direct posting. Before posting we query the creator info (privacy options, maximum video length) as TikTok requires.

We use these scopes only for the SixStar Replays account. We do not read followers, comments, messages, analytics or any other TikTok user's data.

How we use the YouTube API

GTA6 Clips Daily uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload approved clips to the SixStar Replays channel and to delete a clip we uploaded when a takedown is requested. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

What we never do

  • Clip streamers who are not on the allow-list or who asked us not to.
  • Post a clip without the streamer's name and Twitch link.
  • Use unofficial tools, bots or automated browsers to post — only the official TikTok and YouTube APIs.
  • Post clips with music to TikTok.
  • Exceed daily posting limits.
